"Earth Analytics"

What is "Earth Analytics"? I just, kind of, came up with it and it seemed to fit. To me, Earth Analytics is the intersection between Earth Science and Computer Science. That is the niche I want to narrow in on.

This blog will be one key component of my journey to establish a niche. I will use it to highlight interesting projects and establish a portfolio that will be helpful to prospective consulting clients. However, I also want the blog to be useful to others with similar interests and goals. From that perspective, I will try and not only describe what I have done, but how I did it.

In the coming months (and years?) I hope to post about a wide range of topics:

  • Environmental data quality control
  • API development
  • Snow modelling
  • Inverse theory

If any of this interests you follow me on Twitter. Of course feel free to contact me as well.
